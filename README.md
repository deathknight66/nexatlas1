# 🏛️ NexAtlas AI

> Traditional dashboards explain what happened. NexAtlas simulates why it happened, evaluates risks, and recommends strategic actions based on AI executive deliberation.

NexAtlas AI is an AI-powered enterprise digital twin and advisory platform built upon the MiroFish architecture. It transforms a general-purpose social simulation engine into a corporate intelligence ecosystem where autonomous AI agents collaborate to analyze business strategies, data capabilities, IT governance, and digital transformation scenarios.

---

### 🚀 Core Advisory Domains

* **Business Strategy & Operations:** Macro strategic alignment, market anomaly responsiveness, and corporate crisis mitigation modeling.
* **Data Analytics & Business Intelligence:** Structural data quality verification, DAMA-DMBOK data maturity standards auditing, and field execution tracking (*Total Actual Coverage* metrics).
* **IT Governance, Risk & Compliance:** COBIT 2019 framework mapping, data lineage validation, and security audit trail enforcement.
* **Digital Transformation & Technology Strategy:** Enterprise architecture optimization (TOGAF), event-driven streaming pipelines (Apache Kafka), and advanced time-series forecasting model deployment (Hybrid LSTM / CNN-LSTM networks).
